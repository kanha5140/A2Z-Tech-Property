About A2ZTechProperty

A2ZTechProperty is a technology-driven real estate platform developed by BODS Infratech Private Limited. We connect property buyers, sellers, developers, contractors, technical vehicle owners, vendors, and investors on one trusted digital platform.

About BODS Infratech Private Limited

BODS Infratech Private Limited is a registered Indian company incorporated on 03 January 2024 and based in Rourkela, Odisha. We are a Startup India certified organization with a clear mission: to simplify and digitize the real estate and construction ecosystem.

The Real Estate Challenge

The real estate industry in India is highly fragmented. Customers struggle to find trusted agents, developers face marketing and lead challenges, contractors and technical vehicle owners lack consistent work, and transparency is often missing.

Our Solution

We built A2ZTechProperty to solve these problems by creating one unified digital ecosystem where every stakeholder works together on a single platform. A2ZTechProperty brings everything under one roof — Property listings, construction services, technical vehicles, materials, verified partners, and digital lead management.
